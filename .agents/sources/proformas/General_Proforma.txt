H/O presenting complaints : ➢  Visual symptoms :  • Diminution of vision Onset , duration, progression, for distance or near or both, painful/ painless and other associated complaints, unilateral / bilateral • Coloured halos • Glare • Floaters • Photopsia/ Flashes of light • Micropsia/ Macropsia/Metamorphopsia • Diplopia/ Polyopia • Night blindness ( Nyctalopia ) or Day blindness ( Hameralopia ) • Frequent change of glasses  ➢  Non – Visual symptoms/ Symptoms due to involvement  of ocular surface  •  Redness • Watering 

• Discharge • Itching • Foreign body sensation • Irritation  • Dryness • Intolerence to bright light ( photophobia ) • Pain in the eye • Pain with eye movements (optic neuritis) • Discolouration over the surface of the eye ( Conjunctiva or cornea ) • Growth in and around the eye ➢  Symptoms due to involvement of adnexal structures: • Drooping of eyelids ( ptosis ) • Retraction of the eyelid (Lid retraction) • Forward protrusion of the eye or inward displacement of the eye (Proptosis and Enophthalmus) • Lid edema • Skin lesions over the lid or brow • Discolouration of eyelashes or eyebrows (Poliosis) • Loss of eyebrows (Madarosis) ➢  Symptoms caused by anamolies of ocular motility: • Asthenopia/ Feeling of tiredness 

• Binocular diplopia (diplopia disappears when either of the eye is closed) • Squinting / inward or outward turning of one or both the eyes • Headache ➢  Others : • H/O trauma • H/O surgery • H/O DM or HTN 9. Past history : • H/O DM • H/O HTN • H/O usage of any long term medications - steroid, miotic eyedrops  • H/O previous surgeries • H/O use of topical medications  ( especially in cases of glaucoma ) • H/O similar complaints in the other eye • H/O use of glasses for distance / near vision 10. Family history: • Similar complaints in the family  • Any H/O infective diseases in family members 11. Personal history:

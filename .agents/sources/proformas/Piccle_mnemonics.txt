Peripheral cyanosisDue to slow blood flow / increased oxygen extraction in peripheral tissues, while arterial oxygen saturation may be normal.Seen in:* Peripheral circulatory failure/shock* Congestive cardiac failure* Cold exposure* Peripheral vascular disease* Raynaud phenomenonSites:! Fingers, toes, nail beds are blue, but tongue is usually normal.Key point: Tongue is pink + fingers blue → think peripheral cyanosis.

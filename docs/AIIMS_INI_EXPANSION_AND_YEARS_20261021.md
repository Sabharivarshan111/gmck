## Year semantics and compatibility

- The list and question reader display, for example, `AIIMS PG 2017 · November · Recall`, `INI-CET 2022 · May · Recall` or `NEET-PG 2024 · Shift 1 · Recall`.
- `examYear` is separate from the MBBS study-year used for chapter filtering.
- AIIMS/INI dates come from the per-item source ledger; sessions are set only where identified. Unknown 01/02 collection numbers are not guessed as May/November.
- The NEET sidecar is generated by joining stable bundled IDs to the existing source-receipt records, not by parsing arbitrary imported IDs. It applies only in the bundled NEET loader. Legacy/private imports with no explicit metadata display `Year unavailable`.
- Recall wording, source labels and clinical accuracy have practical limits. A source-labelled year is not official exam authentication. Admission January/July years must not be substituted for examination November/May dates.
- Optional metadata preserves old private-bank compatibility; malformed years/sessions are rejected before installation. Search includes available year/session text.
- Bank identity and question IDs remain stable so progress and bookmarks persist. Existing private-import pointer handling and NEET payload are unchanged.

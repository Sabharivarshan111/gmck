# AIIMS PG / INI-CET paper coverage audit — 22 October 2026

## Why the app totals are smaller

The current app has **2,078 NEET PG, 90 AIIMS PG and 161 INI-CET questions**. The AIIMS/INI import is a partial reviewed selection, not a complete previous-paper archive. Its release receipt lists 98 source documents, 76 contributing selections and 2,277 historical extracted candidates. That candidate count is neither a new discovery nor a count of unique, complete, clinically checked or reusable questions.

Only text-only, single-answer selections have been promoted so far. Missing figures/options, multi-answer formats, disputed keys, duplicate recalls, uncertain exam identity and date conflicts require separate work. Whole commercial coaching banks also cannot be presumed reusable merely because their PDF downloads are public. The low totals are principally unfinished source-to-question coverage, not evidence that only 251 questions exist.

The AglaSem 2022 index links 37 subject PDFs (18 May, 19 November). Only 20 document identities were in the existing import source manifest. **17 were absent**. Its3-page original PDF was acquired.

**New acquisition:17 PDFs,139 pages. Newly approved/imported questions:0.** These are audit inputs; their pages are not question counts. No runtime banks were edited in this research pass.

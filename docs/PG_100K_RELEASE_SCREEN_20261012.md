# 100,000-question release screening — 12 October 2026

The owner requested a 100,000-question practice bank while avoiding legal trouble. All 186,930 collected records were scanned, with a reproducible 100,000-record subset selected by sorted record ID. This subset consists of training/general-medical records. It is not a random sample or a verified NEET-PG PYQ bank.

## New licence evidence

On 28 November 2022, repository member monk1337 stated that the team finalized MIT for the current **dataset**: https://github.com/medmcqa/medmcqa/issues/2#issuecomment-1329300257 . The repository licence is https://github.com/medmcqa/medmcqa/blob/main/LICENSE.md . This corrects earlier descriptions treating MIT as applying only to code. MIT permits commercial use and modification of covered material subject to preserving its notice. The Hugging Face mirror continues to declare Apache-2.0. Record these differing declarations; do not silently remove attribution or claim the collected records are ORBIT originals.

Neither licence warrants noninfringement. The scan has not independently established each underlying publisher/examination rights chain. This is an evidence gap, not a finding that all records are unlawful. Scanning, paraphrasing, excluding keywords or changing an exam-year label cannot issue legal clearance.

Absence of these signals does not prove permission or correctness. There are zero independently medically reviewed answers and zero independently verified per-item underlying rights chains in this audit.

The reproducible script is scripts/audit-pg-release-rights.py. The JSON report includes ID-order hashes and aggregate counts, without question stems, options, keys or textbook passages. No new public question distribution or change to the existing original-only release was made by this audit. A bulk release has not been represented as legal or criminal immunity.

## Concrete release routes

1. Use covered dataset material with its required attribution/notices only after resolving the remaining underlying-content permission uncertainty to the owner's required risk standard.
2. Obtain an authorized publisher/rights-holder bank with commercial app and offline redistribution rights.
3. Independently author practice questions from medical concepts, with source-grounded explanations and medical review. Do not disguise recalled questions as independently authored questions or inflate the count with cosmetic variants.

No lawyer is hired, no fee is incurred, and no message is sent to any third party by this screening task. The existing app subscription is unchanged.

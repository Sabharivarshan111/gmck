# Historical INI-CET and legacy AIIMS PG coverage audit — 12 October 2026

The requested bank needs examination attribution for each question, from the beginning of each examination through the latest sitting. The existing general practice corpus and a list of source links do not satisfy that requirement. This audit identifies actual accessible question and answer files, verifies their numbering and records the unresolved attribution gaps. No new questions were imported by this research task.

## Examination chronology

INI-CET begins with the **20 November 2020 examination for January 2021 admission**, not with an assumed 2021 start. The [official JIPMER-hosted admission notice](https://jipmer.edu.in/sites/default/files/NOTICE%201%20-%20%20MD-MS%20January%202021%20%28INI-CET%29.pdf) names that examination date. Legacy AIIMS PG remains a separate exam family. A publisher's admission-year title must not replace the year in which a candidate actually sat the examination.

The common “May 2021” recall label needs correction: the July 2021 admission examination was postponed and held **22 July 2021**. [Prepp's 2021 page](https://prepp.in/ini-cet-exam/question-paper-2021) explicitly distinguishes that date from 14 November 2021 for January 2022 admission; the [contemporaneous report](https://www.hindustantimes.com/education/competitive-exams/aiims-inicet-2021-exam-postponed-check-new-schedule-on-aiimsexamsacin-101624329026401.html) agrees. Preserve the original publisher alias, but display the actual exam month after official date evidence is attached.

## Legacy AIIMS PG: earlier than the modern INI-CET name

[EducationObserver's 2012 thread](https://www.educationobserver.com/forum/showthread.php?tid=12410) provides an actual **November 1999** [26-page solved recall PDF](https://www.educationobserver.com/forum/attachment.php?aid=5209) and **May 2001** [27-page question/answer PDF](https://www.educationobserver.com/forum/attachment.php?aid=5208). November 1999 answers include textbook editions/pages and explicit uncertainty markers. Some stems/options are incomplete; a cited book page alone does not prove the supplied key or the exam attribution.

[Its later archive](https://www.educationobserver.com/forum/showthread.php?tid=17508) lists files for 2000–2013 plus further 2014 links, with several named months. However, the allegedly solved **AIIMS PG 2000** [attachment](https://www.educationobserver.com/forum/attachment.php?aid=12977) is only **two pages and four anatomy questions**. That is a confirmed example of a “year paper” title concealing a fragment. Many older search hits called “AIIMS 1994/1998” are MBBS undergraduate entrance papers and must not enter the PG bank.

The primary [MedMCQA paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v174/pal22a/pal22a.pdf) describes AIIMS PG test-set collection from **1991–present at the time of dataset construction**. It does not supply per-item exam-year/session fields. It cannot justify assigning 1991 to any individual unlabelled record, nor “present” meaning 2026. Earliest actual full-era boundary for the historic AIIMS entrance exam has not been proved by this audit; 1999 is the earliest specific publicly accessible PG recall PDF inspected here, not a claim that the exam started in 1999.

## Sources rejected as coverage evidence

- [CollegeDunia “INI CET 2025 Question Paper with Solutions” PDF](https://image-static.collegedunia.com/public/image/INI_CET_2025_a39c60785a6e950d82d29fc4cb542907.pdf): 11 pages; header says 60 questions/100 marks and allows calculators/chemical equations, incompatible with an authentic 200-question INI-CET paper. Month is absent and its renal endocrine explanation also requires correction. Treat as untrusted practice, not November 2025 PYQs.
- [MyKollege answer-key listing](https://www.mykollege.in/exam/ini-cet/answer-key): many allegedly distinct providers/sessions share the same download target. Listing a link repeatedly cannot establish separate answer keys or session coverage.
- [PrepLadder strategy page](https://www.prepladder.com/neet-pg-study-material/preparation-strategy/ini-cet-pyq-strategy-before-exam): says INI-CET began in 2021 while its own table lists November 2020; official historical notice takes precedence. Download labels render as text rather than actionable PDF links in this retrieval.
- Reflex explicitly adds original “high-yield” questions among recalls and omits unavailable-image questions. Those additions are practice items even when placed beneath an exam-year heading.

## Per-question attribution that can be implemented accurately

For each legitimately usable item, retain `exam_family`, `exam_year` (actual sitting), `exam_month`, `admission_year`, `admission_month`, `source_url`, `source_file_sha256`, `source_page`, `source_item_number`, `publisher_session_label`, `attribution_evidence`, `attribution_status`, `answer_set`, `answer_evidence`, `visual_status`, and `rights_evidence`. Numbering is source numbering, never falsely labelled as original examination order.

Example metadata without reproducing content: Prepp 2020 `source_item_number=1`, `exam_year=2020`, `exam_month=11`, `admission_year=2021`, `admission_month=1`, `attribution_status=publisher_recall`, source page 1, paired key Q1. For Prepp 2021 set 02 Q1, retain the publisher's May alias and the actual July date only with corroboration; remaining uncorroborated entries remain year-only. A content match against a general practice bank is a candidate attribution, not proof of an exact repeat.

This research establishes real earlier question/answer files and actionable item-level attribution opportunities. It does **not** establish complete all-session coverage or perfect answers; missing sessions, unknown month mappings, images, duplicates, key errors and reuse rights are concrete remaining work.

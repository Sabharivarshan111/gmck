# Historical NEET-PG and AIPGMEE coverage audit — 12 October 2026

The requested deliverable is an attributed question archive from commencement through 2026, with each question's examination, year and session. The existing undated practice dataset does not satisfy it. This document records inspected evidence and gaps; it does not claim those questions were imported or medically verified. No copyrighted question bank text is reproduced here.

## Correct historical boundary

The [Government of India announcement, 26 September 2012](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=87979) identifies the first NEET-PG as the 2013–14 admission examination, held on 23 November–6 December 2012. Store **admission year 2013** separately from **test calendar year 2012**. A publisher's “NEET-PG 2012” title alone cannot distinguish that examination from AIPGMEE 2012.

The [Government announcement, 21 September 2016](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=150952) identifies AIPGMEE admissions during 2014–2016 and NEET-PG 2017 examinations on 5–13 December 2016, with 300 questions. Thus the archive must distinguish NEET-PG 2013, AIPGMEE 2014–2016 and NEET-PG 2017 onward. Older AIPGMEE and independent AIIMS PG papers belong in separate historical collections; they must not be renamed NEET-PG. Exact individual dates/shifts within multi-day CBT windows remain unknown unless supported at question level.

## 2026 official-key investigation and rights

An indexed [NBEMS notice index](https://webdisk.natboard.edu.in/allnotice.php) lists “Scorecard and Answer Key of NEET-PG 2026” on 1 October 2026; direct retrieval failed. It needs the actual official notice and access/reuse terms before claiming public downloadable full questions or authoritative keys. This finding invalidates a blanket, unqualified claim that NBEMS never releases answers. Candidate access and public redistribution are separate questions.

A [4 September 2026 report](https://indianexpress.com/article/education/neet-pg-2026-nbems-warns-against-sharing-recall-based-questions-10860518/) records an NBEMS warning about circulating 2026 recall questions under the bulletin's NDA. This is a reported institutional restriction, not a legal conclusion about every historical question. Obtain the actual bulletin/notice and establish reuse rights per source before redistribution. Merely locating a public PDF does not grant republication rights. Original app code licensing is separate from dataset licensing and underlying question ownership.

Do not relabel AIPGMEE predecessors as modern NEET-PG without preserving their original examination identity. Keep 2024 shifts separate; keep the 2026 main and re-examination groups separate. The user-supplied FirstRanker archive is audited in a separate source report.

Recommended ingestion record: original examination name; examination date; admission/exam session; provider and exact source URL; source item ID; stem/options/image completeness; supplied answer text and option index; independently checked textbook passage and edition/page or paragraph; disagreement status; reviewer status; rights evidence. Keep unsupported answers provisional, with no verified badge.

Observed source-quality failures: the PrepLadder archive contains a dermatology-style stem attached to neuroanatomical options under an anatomy heading. Prepp’s 2024 article describes a single shift while its own download table lists two shifts. These are directly observed document defects, not a medical assertion that every answer on either website is wrong.

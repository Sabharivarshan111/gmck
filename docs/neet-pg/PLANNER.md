# NEET-PG daily practice

ORBIT now offers a four-step optional planner in Vault → NEET-PG → Build your NEET-PG study plan. It asks for a personal goal, optional exam date, practice minutes, weekly study days, preparation stage and up to three priority subjects. Library access remains available without onboarding.

The plan selects real records from the active bank, using existing chapter taxonomy and immutable options, supplied answers, explanations and illustrations. At an estimated two minutes per question, a session allocates up to half its slots to due review, then adds new questions with priority subjects first. More due reviews fill otherwise empty slots. Session IDs and position persist in order; sessions survive closing and reloading. Preparation stage and rank goal are personal preferences, not predictions.

The optional exam-date estimate counts selected study weekdays and compares remaining first-pass questions with the time budget. It explicitly allows for additional review time and does not claim complete syllabus coverage. Off-days permit optional practice. Answer reveals without a choice are unscored. Latest scored answers determine displayed accuracy. Correct answers return after 1, 3, 7 and 14 days; missed or unscored questions return after one day. Reopening an explanation on the same day cannot increase the review streak.

A new NEET-PG question-of-the-day page appears in the Home widget carousel beside the existing MCQ and picture pages. Selection is deterministic by local date and active bank, and opens the exact question in the same offline reader. Imported private banks are respected. Questions/options/keys/explanations are not generated by this widget.

Plans and practice history are stored per bank on the current device. There is no new cross-device sync promise or notification scheduler. Native bundled practice is offline. The web app needs its initial load/cache before offline use. Ask ACEV remains contextual and requires internet.

## Design

The supplied competitor screenshots informed general patterns: concise setup, time budgets, priority subjects, daily practice and contextual explanation. The implementation uses ORBIT’s own tokens, Roboto, theme palette, components and existing bank. No competitor branding, wording, brain illustrations, research data, rank guarantees or exact screen composition was reused.

W3C’s multi-page form guidance informed explicit progress and back navigation: https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/multi-page/ . This app’s review intervals are product choices rather than a claim to reproduce any study’s protocol or results.

## Verification

`node --experimental-strip-types scripts/test-neet-pg-plan.mjs` checks local dates/leap years, deterministic daily selection, review spacing, same-day reveal safety, capacity, subject priority, malformed storage and exam-date workload calculations.

`CHROME_PATH=<chromium> node mobile/web/neet-pg-plan-check.mjs` drives the built actual application: optional setup, invalid dates, plan save, answer reveal, bookmarks, contextual ACEV entry, ordered resume after reload, daily question offline reload, and reader controls at 360/390/768/1024 widths. Captures used by the Remotion ad come from this real flow; profile setup alone is seeded. It does not certify native hardware or the online AI service.

The existing offline import/replacement regression, immutable answer checks, typecheck and lint remain release gates. The candidate workflow verifies the exact branch SHA before dispatching fresh signed/internal/debug builds; the main-branch anatomy certification requirement remains intact.
